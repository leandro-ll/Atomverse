# Chemistry Seed Authoring Guide

This document defines the authoring standards, validation rules, review boundaries, and governance protocols for all chemistry seeds located in `catalog/seed/`.

---

## 1. Overview and Philosophy

To prevent maintenance overhead and impossible review burdens, Atomverse separates **human-curated seeds** from **machine-generated artifacts**:

- **Human-curated seeds** (`catalog/seed/`): Compact, verified, and strictly budgeted (~70 total authored rows across 4 files).
- **Machine-generated catalog** (`catalog/chemistry.catalog.json`): Comprehensive, deterministically compiled dataset containing ~99 substances and ~990 reaction records generated by `scripts/catalog/generate.py`.

Audit task 3.1 is explicitly satisfied through deterministic generation from these seeds rather than manual entry.

---

## 2. The Four Seed Files and the 70-Row Budget

The total authored rows across all seed files must remain within the **~70-row budget** to ensure that every row can be thoroughly reviewed by human domain experts.

| Seed File | Purpose | Approximate Budget | Key Fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| `catalog/seed/ions.json` | Cations and anions with oxidation states, multiplicities, and names | ~20 rows (11 cations, 9 anions) | `id`, `symbol`, `charge`, `type`, `name`, `multiplicity` |
| `catalog/seed/solubility-rules.json` | Ordered qualitative solubility heuristics with exceptions | ~10 rules | `rule_id`, `description`, `solubility`, `ions`, `exceptions`, `citation` |
| `catalog/seed/ksp.json` | Quantitative solubility product constants for sparingly soluble salts | ~20 rows | `formula`, `ksp`, `temperature_c`, `citation`, `transcribedBy`, `checkedBy` |
| `catalog/seed/presentation.json` | UI display metadata, hex colors, and localized naming overrides | ~20 rows | `substance_id`, `color_hex`, `color_name_id`, `display_name_id` |

---

## 3. Ordered Solubility-Rule Semantics

Solubility classification for binary ionic compounds uses an ordered list of heuristics:

1. **First-Match-Wins Hierarchy**:
   - The generator iterates through rules in exact array order (from rule 1 to rule $N$).
   - The first rule whose ion conditions match the target substance determines the default solubility (`soluble` or `insoluble`).
2. **In-Rule Exceptions**:
   - Each rule specifies a list of ion exceptions.
   - If a substance matches the main ion criteria but also matches an item in `exceptions`, the classification is inverted (e.g. rule: "All chlorides ($\text{Cl}^-$) are soluble", exception: $\text{Ag}^+$, $\text{Pb}^{2+} \implies \text{AgCl}$ and $\text{PbCl}_2$ are insoluble).
3. **Deterministic Precedence**:
   - Group 1 cations ($\text{Li}^+$, $\text{Na}^+$, $\text{K}^+$) and nitrate ($\text{NO}_3^-$) rules appear first, ensuring universal solubility rules take precedence over lower anion rules.

---

## 4. $K_{sp}$ Transcription and Two-Person Review Rules

All quantitative solubility data must satisfy strict integrity constraints:

1. **Authored Decimal Strings**:
   - $K_{sp}$ values **must** be stored as authored decimal strings in scientific or decimal notation (e.g. `"1.77e-10"`, `"1.7e-5"`).
   - Values **must never** be parsed into or emitted from IEEE-754 binary floating-point numbers during compilation or transcription, preserving exact significant digits and avoiding round-trip drift.
2. **Mandatory Citations**:
   - Every row must include a verifiable literature citation (e.g., CRC Handbook of Chemistry and Physics 97th ed., Lange's Handbook of Chemistry, or IUPAC Solubility Data Series).
3. **Distinct Reviewer Requirement (`transcribedBy` $\ne$ `checkedBy`)**:
   - Each entry must record:
     - `transcribedBy`: The author who transcribed the value and citation.
     - `checkedBy`: A distinct reviewer who independently cross-checked the transcription against the cited physical source.
   - Committing a row where `transcribedBy == checkedBy` or where `checkedBy` is missing triggers automated validation failure.

---

## 5. Offline AI Drafting Boundary

When utilizing Large Language Models or AI assistance for seed maintenance:

1. **Permissible AI Assistance**:
   - AI drafting may propose candidate substance names, educational pedagogical descriptions, and preliminary $K_{sp}$ candidate lists.
2. **Prohibited AI Authority**:
   - **No AI output is authoritative at runtime**: Neither frontend nor backend engines invoke LLMs to determine reaction feasibility, equations, solubility, or masses.
   - **Mandatory Deterministic Validation**: All chemical formulas, stoichiometric charges, and mass balances must be checked by the deterministic build toolkit (`scripts/catalog/generate.py` and ChemPy).
   - **Human Bibliographic Verification**: AI-generated $K_{sp}$ citations must be manually verified against physical references by human reviewers before addition to `ksp.json`.
